## What they are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser's local storage) keep small pieces of information on your device. They make the website work and, with consent, measure campaigns.

## What we use

| Category | Purpose | Name | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Necessary (always on) | Remember your cookie choice | `hp-consent` (local storage) | 12 months |
| Necessary (always on) | Carry the visit's source to the application, within the same visit | `hp-touch` (session storage) | Until the browser is closed |
| Analytics (with consent) | Measure visits and how the website is used (Google Analytics 4) | `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Up to 24 months |
| Advertising (with consent) | Measure and optimize ads (Meta) | `_fbp`, `_fbc` | Up to 3 months |
| Advertising (with consent) | Measure and optimize ads (Google Ads) | `_gcl_au` | Up to 3 months |
| Analytics or advertising (with consent) | Keep the visit's source between visits | `hp-attribution` (local storage) | 90 days |

## Consent mode

The website uses Google Consent Mode v2. Until consent is given, analytics and advertising tools do not store cookies on your device.

## How to change your choice

Use the "Cookie preferences" button on this page or in the website footer. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
